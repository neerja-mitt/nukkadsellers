How a live drop works

Run it as an auction, a fixed-price drop, or a mix of both.

  1. Schedule your drop

    Pick a time. Your followers and our buyers get a reminder before you go live.

  2. Show each piece

    Size, condition and any flaws, all on camera. Answer questions as they come in.

  3. Buyers bid or buy

    Set a starting price for each piece. Highest bid when the timer ends wins it.

  4. Pack and ship

    Winners check out right away. You get one order list. We arrange pickup.

Questions

Where can I sell thrift and vintage clothes online in India?

Most thrift stores sell through Instagram drops, Reddit communities and resale apps. Nukkad lets you run those drops live: show each piece on camera, flaws and all, take bids or fixed prices, and sell to buyers across India. It is open to thrift stores, vintage curators and resellers of pre-loved clothing, bags, silver, home pieces and collectibles.

How do live thrift auctions work?

You set a starting price for each piece and show it on camera. Buyers bid while a timer counts down, the highest bid wins, and the winner checks out on the spot. You can also sell at fixed prices, or mix both in one show.

Do I have to run auctions?

No. You can run fixed-price drops, auctions, or a mix. Many stores auction their best pieces and price the rest.

Can I set a starting price?

Yes. You set the starting price for every piece, so nothing goes for less than you're happy with.

Do I need GST to apply?

Not to apply. We'll talk through what you need before your first show.

What happens to pieces that don't sell?

They stay in your Nukkad shop at a fixed price, so buyers who missed the show can still get them.

Is there a fee to apply?

No. Applying is free, and so is the call.

How do you make money?

We take a share of what sells. We'll explain the terms on the call, before you commit to anything.
